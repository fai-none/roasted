# Two-call demo rehearsal

Use the signed Roasted app on the connected iPhone, with the Mac HTTPS broker running and both devices on a reachable network. Home must say **HIGGS LIVE**. The mock walkthrough is a separate sample and cannot prove learning memory.

1. Open Home and read the AI-agents debate. Note the current memory summary; do not seed a mistake into the database.
2. Tap **Get roasted**, then **Accept**. Allow microphone/local-network access. Confirm you hear Nobody and it responds to what you actually say.
3. Give your honest opinion. If useful for demonstrating correction, intentionally make a real spoken mistake such as “Humans can spend time for more important things.” This is a rehearsal suggestion, not a saved fixture. React to the roast and retry the wording Nobody actually offers.
4. Continue the argument. Try a Thai word when you cannot find the English one. Interrupt once, then use mute/unmute. Check that old speech stops and Nobody follows your new thought.
5. Let the correction finish, then retry. Wait for the retry caption to finish before ending; interrupt a separate conversational turn, because interrupted speech cannot be trusted as correction evidence. End after a complete exchange. The receipt must say **Saved in InsForge**. Compare its quoted original, offered alternative and retry with what was actually said. Empty fields are preferable to invented evidence.
6. Return Home, fully close and reopen the app, and open that saved receipt. Confirm its examples match. Start a new call; Nobody must use at least one saved mistake or progress signal naturally. The second call retrieves InsForge again.
7. End the second call. Confirm the microphone indicator stops and another call can start normally.

Record the device/build/date, receipt ID, retrieved signal and exact call-two callback. Distinguish tester observations from founder acceptance. Do not record raw audio or copy credentials into evidence.
